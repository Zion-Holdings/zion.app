## Part 2: Free Lead Generation Methods & Tools

### Method 1: LinkedIn Free Search + Outreach
- Use LinkedIn free search with filters: industry, company size, job title
- Send personalized connection requests (no InMail needed)
- Follow up with value-first message

### Method 2: Google Maps / Local Search
- Search "IT services [city]" or "managed IT [city]"
- Extract business names, find emails via company websites
- Cold email or call

### Method 3: GitHub Open Source Engagement
- Find companies with public repos in your niche
- Contribute to issues, mention services in profile
- Build relationships before pitching

### Method 4: Content + SEO
- Publish blog posts targeting service keywords
- Share on LinkedIn, Twitter, relevant communities
- Capture leads via contact form / newsletter

### Method 5: Free Tools as Lead Magnets
- Offer free tools (JSON formatter, base64 encoder, etc.) that capture emails
- Use tool usage as qualification signal

### Free Tools to Use
- **Apollo.io** — free tier: 50 credits/month for email finding
- **Hunter.io** — free tier: 25 searches/month
- **Snov.io** — free tier: limited credits
- **LinkedIn** — free search + connection requests
- **Google Maps** — free business discovery
- **GitHub** — free repo search + engagement
- **Crunchbase** — free tier for company discovery
- **Twitter/X Advanced Search** — free, find companies discussing needs
