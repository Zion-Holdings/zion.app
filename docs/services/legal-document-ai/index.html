AI Legal Document Analysis

Analyze, classify, and extract information from legal documents — contracts, briefs, filings, NDAs, and regulatory documents — with AI that surfaces key terms, risks, and obligations.

Legal Documents Are Dense and Time-Consuming

Lawyers and legal teams spend enormous time reading, classifying, and extracting information from documents. Contracts need review. Briefs need analysis. Regulatory filings need tracking. Discovery produces thousands of documents that need categorization. Most of this work is repetitive reading and note-taking — exactly the kind of work AI can assist with.

What the Service Does

  • Document classification — Automatically classify documents by type: contracts, pleadings, motions, NDAs, letters, regulatory filings, and more. Route them to the right workflow.
  • Key clause and term extraction — Identify and extract important terms: effective dates, termination rights, payment terms, confidentiality obligations, indemnification clauses, and jurisdiction. Surface what matters without reading every page.
  • Risk flagging — Flag non-standard terms, missing protections, and clauses that deviate from your playbook. Prioritize what lawyers need to review.
  • Semantic search — Search legal document collections by meaning, not just keywords. Find documents that discuss a concept even when they use different language.
  • Summarization — Produce summaries of long documents: what the document is, what it requires, what it gives, and what the key risks are.
  • Due diligence support — In M&A and financing, accelerate document review by pre-classifying, extracting key terms, and flagging issues for attorney review.

Important Boundaries

AI legal document analysis assists legal professionals — it does not replace legal judgment. All outputs are reviewed by qualified lawyers. The system is designed to accelerate review and improve consistency, not to make autonomous legal decisions.
