AI Support Automation: Metrics That Matter

Most teams measure automation rate. The useful signals are outcome quality and cost per resolution.

Primary metrics

Resolution rate, reopen rate, CSAT, and time-to-resolution. These tell you if customers are actually helped.

Cost metrics

Cost per resolved contact, tool-call success rate, and escalation rate. These tell you if automation is cheap.

Quality metrics

Answer accuracy, citation rate, and policy adherence. These tell you if automation is safe.
