Step-by-Step: Building the Pipeline

1

Define Your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

Before any automation, define who you're targeting. This feeds every downstream step. Your ICP should include:

  • Industry / vertical (e.g., "Brazilian fintechs with $5M-$50M ARR")
  • Company size (employees, revenue)
  • Technology stack signals (e.g., "uses AWS, has a public API, uses React")
  • Geographic focus
  • Decision-maker titles (e.g., "CTO, VP Engineering, Head of Product")
Pro tip: Store your ICP as a structured document in Notion or Google Docs, and have your AI agent reference it for every lead scoring decision. This keeps scoring consistent.
2

Set Up Discovery Automation

The first step is finding prospects. You have several options in 2026:

  • Firecrawl: Scrape competitor sites, industry directories, and review platforms (G2, Capterra) for company lists. Firecrawl handles JavaScript-heavy sites that traditional scrapers can't.
  • Browser Tool: For sites that require interaction (login walls, infinite scroll, maps), use browser automation to extract prospect data.
  • Composio Search / Tavily / Exa: AI-powered search to find companies matching your ICP criteria.
  • LinkedIn (via Composio): Search for companies and decision-makers matching your ICP.

3

Research & Enrich Each Prospect

Once you have a prospect list, enrich each one with deep research. This is where AI agents shine — they can research faster and more thoroughly than a human analyst:

  • Company background: Funding history, investors, founding date, growth stage
  • Tech stack: What technologies they use (BuiltWith, Wappalyzer, or AI inference from their site)
  • Recent news: Funding rounds, product launches, leadership changes, partnerships
  • Decision-makers: Names, titles, LinkedIn profiles of key contacts
  • Intent signals: Hiring for relevant roles, recent investments, technology deployments

4

Score and Prioritize Leads

Not all leads are equal. Score each prospect against your ICP to prioritize outreach:

SignalScore WeightExample
ICP fit (industry, size, tech)40%Fintech, 50-200 employees, uses AWS
Intent signals25%Hiring for engineering roles, recent funding
Decision-maker accessibility20%CTO profile found on LinkedIn, direct email pattern identified
Engagement potential15%Recent blog posts, active social presence, community participation

Leads scoring above your threshold get immediate outreach. Borderline leads go into a nurture sequence. Low-scoring leads are archived.

5

Personalized Multi-Channel Outreach

This is where AI personalization at scale beats manual outreach. For each high-scoring lead, generate personalized outreach across channels:

  • Email (Gmail via Composio): Reference specific research — "I saw your recent Series B and noticed you're using AWS. We helped a similar fintech reduce cloud costs by 40%..."
  • LinkedIn: Send connection requests with personalized notes referencing shared interests or recent activity
  • WhatsApp Business: For markets where WhatsApp is primary (Brazil, LATAM, India), send concise, personalized messages
The personalization formula that works in 2026: Research finding + Relevant Zion capability + Social proof + Low-friction CTA.

Example: "Saw [Company] just raised $20M Series B (TechCrunch, Aug 2026). Congrats. We helped [Similar Company] cut cloud costs 40% post-funding. Worth a 15-min chat to explore if similar savings apply? [Calendly link]"
6

CRM Sync & Automated Follow-Up

Every interaction must flow back into your CRM automatically. This is where most manual processes break down — sales reps forget to update CRM, data goes stale, follow-ups get missed.

  • HubSpot / Airtable / Notion: Record every lead, enrichment, score, outreach sent, response received, and outcome
  • Automated follow-up sequences: If no response in 3 days, send a follow-up. If no response in 7 days, send a different angle. If no response in 14 days, move to nurture.
  • Slack alerts: When a lead responds or books a meeting, alert the sales team immediately via Slack
  • Linear / project tracking: Track follow-up tasks, meeting prep, and post-meeting actions

7

Measure, Optimize, Scale

Track these metrics to optimize your pipeline:

  • Discovery yield: How many prospects discovered → how many match ICP
  • Research depth: Time spent per prospect research (AI should be minutes, not hours)
  • Response rate: Emails getting replies (target: 15-30% for well-personalized outreach)
  • Meeting booking rate: Responses that convert to meetings (target: 20-40% of replies)
  • Pipeline generated: Total pipeline value from automated outreach per month
  • Cost per qualified lead: Total tool spend / number of qualified leads (Composio at $0.0003/call makes this very low)
Common failure mode: Building the pipeline but not reviewing metrics. Check your funnel weekly. If discovery yield is low, refine your ICP. If response rate is low, improve personalization. If meeting booking is low, fix your CTA.
