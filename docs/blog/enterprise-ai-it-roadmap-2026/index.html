Enterprise AI + IT Roadmap 2026

Most enterprises underinvest in the integration layer between AI prototypes and IT operations. This roadmap closes that gap.

Q1: baseline

Inventory systems, integrations, data quality, and security posture. Pick one high-value agent use case and measure baseline latency, cost, and success rate.

Q2: ship safely

Deploy with canary traffic, guardrails, and human escalation. Add observability before expanding scope.

Q3: scale

Expand successful patterns, reduce per-request cost, and harden SLAs. Connect IT ops to AI ops with shared alerts and runbooks.

Q4: optimize

Review architecture, retire brittle integrations, and consolidate vendors. Plan the next cycle with clearer success metrics.
