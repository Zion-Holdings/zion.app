Enterprise consultancies often require 4-8 weeks just to scope a pilot. By the time the team is formed, the business window has shifted. For AI where the technology landscape changes monthly, this is a structural disadvantage.

High Cost, High Overhead

Big firm fees reflect partner margins, global infrastructure, and sales cycles. A pilot that costs $100K+ with a big firm can often be done by a focused team for $20-50K — with the same or better outcomes.

Many enterprise AI engagements end at the strategy or pilot phase. The "billable hours" model incentivizes ongoing advisory, not production deployment. Buyers report "PowerPoint pilots" that never reach production.

Vendor Lock-In

Big consultancies often steer you toward their preferred platforms, tools, and implementation partners — limiting your flexibility and increasing long-term dependency.
