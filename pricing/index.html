Frequently Asked Questions

How does billing work?

We offer monthly and annual billing. Annual plans save 20%. All plans include a 14-day free trial. We accept credit cards, bank transfer, and for enterprise clients, net-30 invoicing.

Can I switch plans mid-month?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately with pro-rated billing. Downgrades apply at the next billing cycle. Contact your account manager for custom plan adjustments.

What's included in the free tools?

Our free tools include the AI Service Router, ROI Calculator, Service Comparison, Service Recommender, Port Scanner, and SSL Certificate Checker. No credit card required.

Do you offer custom enterprise pricing?

Yes. Enterprise plans are tailored to your specific needs, including custom AI implementations, multi-team deployments, and volume-based pricing. Book a consultation to discuss your requirements.

How does the 14-day trial work?

Start any paid plan and get full access for 14 days at no cost. No credit card required to start. If you choose to continue, billing starts on day 15. Cancel anytime.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), bank transfers, and for enterprise clients, net-30 invoicing. We use Stripe for secure payment processing.
