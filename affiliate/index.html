Frequently Asked Questions

How long does my referral link stay active?

Your referral link has a 90-day cookie window. If someone clicks your link and signs up within 90 days, you get credited — even if they don't buy immediately.

Can I refer clients from anywhere in the world?

Yes. Zion Tech Group serves clients globally. We handle the language and time zone — you just make the introduction.

When and how do I get paid?

Payouts are processed on the 1st of each month for the previous month's earnings. You can receive payouts via Stripe (direct to your bank) or PayPal. Minimum payout: $50.

Do I need a website to join?

No. You can share your link on social media, in email signatures, in client meetings, on Slack/Discord communities, or anywhere you talk to potential clients. We track conversions from your unique link regardless of where you share it.

What if I refer someone who's already a Zion client?

If they're already a paying client, you don't earn a commission on their existing contracts — but you do earn on any new services or upgrades they purchase through your link.

Is there a minimum number of referrals I need to make?

No minimum. One referral = one commission. There are no quotas, no tiers, and no risk of losing your affiliate status for low activity.
