The CRM Integration Problem

Most B2B enterprises in 2026 run a fragmented tool stack:

  • Leads come in via Gmail, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and web forms
  • Deals are tracked in HubSpot or Salesforce
  • Engineering tasks live in Linear or Jira
  • Documentation is in Notion
  • Communication happens in Slack and email
  • Customer data is scattered across all of the above
The reality: Manual CRM updates don't happen. Data goes stale. Sales reps hate administrative work. And the business makes decisions based on incomplete data because nothing is synchronized.

Zion Tech Group solves this with Composio-powered CRM integrations that automatically sync data across your entire tool stack — no manual updates, no copy-paste, no stale records.

Workflows We Build

  1. Lead Capture → CRM: Lead comes in via Gmail, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, or web form → automatically created in HubSpot/Airtable with full enrichment data.
  2. Lead Enrichment Pipeline: New lead in CRM → AI researches company (funding, tech stack, news) → data added to CRM record → lead scored.
  3. Deal Stage Automation: Deal moves to "proposal" → Linear task created for proposal team → Slack notification sent → Notion client page updated.
  4. Post-Meeting Sync: Meeting ends → notes from Google Docs/Notion → CRM record updated → follow-up tasks created in Linear → Slack alert to account manager.
  5. Customer Health Monitoring: Usage data from your product → CRM updated → Slack alert if engagement drops → account manager notified.
  6. Pipeline Reporting: Weekly pipeline report generated from CRM data → exported to Google Sheets → emailed to sales leadership → Slack summary posted.
