Enterprise Security & Compliance

  • RBAC (Role-Based Access Control): Control which agents and users can access which tools. Granular permissions per app, per action.
  • IP Allowlist: Restrict Composio access to your corporate IP ranges. Enterprise add-on.
  • BAA (Business Associate Agreement): Available for HIPAA-regulated enterprises. Enterprise add-on.
  • ZDR (Zero Trust Data Retention): Enterprise-grade data handling policies. Add-on.
  • KMS Proxy: Bring your own key management for encrypted tool execution. Enterprise.
  • Sandboxed Execution: All tool execution runs in Docker-based sandboxed environments — no tool runs on your infrastructure.
  • Audit Logs: Every tool call is logged with timestamp, user, app, action, and result. 30-day retention on Pro, custom on Enterprise.
  • Spend Controls: Per-meter spend caps on Pro — never exceed your budget. Free tier is hard-capped.
