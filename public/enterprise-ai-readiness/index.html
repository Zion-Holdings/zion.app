Enterprise AI Readiness Assessment

Score your organization across strategy, data, infrastructure, governance, and execution—then prioritize the highest-impact gaps.

1. Strategy and use-case clarity

Assess whether AI investments map to measurable business outcomes.

  • Executive sponsorship and KPIs
  • Prioritized use-case backlog
  • Change management plan

2. Data quality and access

Most AI failures start with data ownership, freshness, and integration issues.

  • Source inventory and lineage
  • Quality controls and validation

3. Infrastructure and security

Cloud, identity, observability, and incident response must be production-grade before scaling AI.

  • Identity, access, and segmentation
  • Monitoring for latency, cost, and errors
  • Runbooks and escalation paths

4. Governance and risk

Regulatory exposure and model risk require documented controls and audit evidence.

  • Model inventory and approval workflow
  • Bias, safety, and accuracy testing
  • Data residency and export controls
