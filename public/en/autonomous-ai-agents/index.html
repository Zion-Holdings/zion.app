Governance — five points before production

The five points below are the production minimum. CIO/CISO depth — risk classes, HITL design, and regulated patterns — expands in Enterprise AI agent governance & risk.

  1. Named owner — business + technical RACI before go-live.
  2. Allowed tools — explicit read/write list; no silent new connectors.
  3. Human gates — irreversible or privileged steps require approval.
  4. Spend envelope — quotas, tags, kill-switch when cost spikes.
  5. Audit trail — logs suitable for internal review and regulated environments.

Enterprise AI agent governance & risk

For CIO, CISO, and CTO buyers, AI agent governance is the product — autonomy without controls is a liability. Chatbot pilots fail quietly. Agents that can write to CRM, tickets, ERP, or email fail loudly.

Risk classes to map

Data — what the agent may read; PII / customer / regulated handling; retention of prompts and tool outputs. Actions — which tools may write; destructive vs reversible; approval gates. Spend — model/API quotas, owner tags, kill-switch (pairs with FinOps). Audit — who can replay a decision path. Ownership — RACI for agent owner, security, platform, and business ops.

Human-in-the-loop design

Default production posture: read + suggest or tightly scoped writes until the workflow earns more autonomy. Explicit escalation when confidence is low, policy is ambiguous, or the action is irreversible. Separated duties: builders do not silently own production allowlists without security review.

Regulated environments — patterns, not fake certs

Zion designs control patterns suitable for stricter industries (e.g. fintech-style change control, healthcare-aware data handling). We do not claim SOC 2, HIPAA, or other certifications on this page. Healthcare BAA / HIPAA-aware ops: HIPAA-compliant IT & AI. Fintech framing: AI & managed IT for fintech.

Helpdesk vs chatbot

Chatbot: answers from a knowledge base; humans still close the work. Helpdesk agent: enriches tickets, suggests category/priority, packages context, routes queues — and only acts within policy.

Safe automation patterns

Triage / enrich / route first. Known-error playbooks with human gates on privileged or irreversible steps (identity resets, access grants, destructive commands). Clear RACI: agent assists L1; humans own exceptions. Cost controls on model/API calls (pairs with FinOps).
