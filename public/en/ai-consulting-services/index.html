Readiness checks before spend

Readiness is not a separate product SKU. It is the pre-spend checklist inside consulting and Discovery — so you do not fund agents or platforms before the operating conditions exist. No /ai-readiness-assessment/ thin URL.

  1. Process clarity — one named workflow with owners and handoffs.
  2. Data / tool access — systems the work actually touches; who can grant access.
  3. Success metric — what “done” means in ops language (not vanity AI demos).
  4. Risk posture — HITL gates, PII rules, spend caps (see agent governance).
  5. Operating model — who runs it after week two (in-house, co-managed IT, or Growth).

Readiness vs ROI: readiness asks “can we execute safely?” ROI asks “should we fund this first?” Discovery covers both when the brief includes stack + process + leadership questions.
