Managed IT for AI-first companies

AI-first mid-market teams (models, agents, or heavy API usage in production) break classic MSP packages: ops, security baselines, and cost must share one operating model.

Why ticket-only MSP packages break

GPU/cloud and model/API spend move faster than seat math. Agents and copilots create new identity, secret, and tool-access surfaces. L1 load shifts toward triage + known-error playbooks — with guardrails, not unsupervised closures. Platform and IT ownership blur without a clear RACI.
