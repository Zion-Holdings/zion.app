AI Automation Patterns That Actually Ship

Most automation fails from brittle branching and missing fallbacks. These patterns keep workflows robust.

1. Normalize first

Convert every input into a canonical shape before branching. It reduces downstream bugs.

2. Retry with backoff

Use bounded retries and clear failure states. Do not retry forever.

2. Observability

Emit one structured event per step. If you cannot measure it, you cannot improve it.

4. Human escalation

Define exact conditions for human handoff. Automate the handoff, not the exception.
