IT Strategy for 2026

IT strategy in 2026 blends platform engineering, AI-assisted operations, and cost governance. This guide helps leaders align infrastructure with business outcomes.

1. Define platform primitives

Standardize identity, networking, observability, and deployment pipelines. Reduce variability to increase team velocity.

2. Apply AI to operations

Use AI for incident triage, log analysis, capacity forecasting, and patch prioritization. Start with high-volume repetitive tasks.

3. Control cloud costs

Implement FinOps practices: budgets, anomaly detection, right-sizing, and reserved capacity planning. Review spend weekly.

4. Strengthen resilience

Design for failure with chaos testing, automated recovery, and multi-region redundancy. Validate runbooks regularly.

5. Modernize selectively

Prioritize modernization by business risk and operational pain. Avoid big-bang replatforming in favor of incremental migration.
