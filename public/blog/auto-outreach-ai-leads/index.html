AI-powered autonomous outreach that researches leads, crafts personalized messages, and manages multi-touch sequences around the clock — without burning sender reputation on duplicate or low-quality sends.

The outreach problem

Most sales teams spend 60-80% of their time on activities that don't convert: researching leads manually, writing personalized outreach from scratch, following up inconsistently, tracking responses in spreadsheets.

And even when they do the work, they're limited by hours in the day. A sales rep can send maybe 50-80 personalized emails a day before quality collapses.

What autonomous outreach agents actually do

  • Research continuously — scan company databases, news, funding rounds, hiring patterns, and technographic signals to build a living lead pool.
  • Craft personalized outreach — write context-aware messages that reference the prospect's actual situation, not just {firstName} templates.
  • Manage multi-touch sequences — send the right message at the right time across email and messaging, with follow-ups that add value instead of pestering.
  • Track and adapt — monitor responses, adjust messaging based on what works, and route qualified leads to the right sales rep.

What this looks like in practice

A mid-market SaaS company with 4 sales reps was generating ~15-20 qualified leads/month through manual outreach. After deploying an autonomous outreach engine on the Hermes Agent Platform:

  • Lead research went from manual to continuous (~500 prospects/day)
  • Outreach became personalized and context-aware
  • Multi-touch sequences ran automatically with value-add follow-ups
  • Qualified lead volume went from 15-20/month to 60-80/month in 60 days

Same 4 reps. 3-4x more pipeline. Agents handled research and admin.
