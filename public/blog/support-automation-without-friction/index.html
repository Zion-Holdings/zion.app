Support Automation Without Friction

Good support automation removes busywork without removing empathy. Start with routing, not replacement.

Triage first

Classify requests by urgency and intent. Route high-touch cases to humans immediately.

Answer with evidence

Use citations and policy snippets instead of generic answers. Trust comes from accuracy.

Measure outcomes

Track resolution time, reopen rate, and CSAT. Automation is successful only if outcomes improve.
