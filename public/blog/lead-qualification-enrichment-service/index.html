The hidden cost of bad-fit leads

Not all leads are created equal. Most prospecting effort ends up on contacts that will never buy — wrong budget, wrong timeline, wrong need, wrong decision-maker.

When your team spends hours researching, crafting, and engaging bad-fit leads, that's time they're not spending on deals that could close.

What Lead Qualification & Enrichment does

We deploy qualification and enrichment agents built on the Hermes Agent Platform that:

  • Score leads continuously — evaluate fit across budget, authority, need, timeline, and other criteria specific to your offering
  • Enrich contact data — fill in missing fields (company size, tech stack, funding, key contacts, recent news)
  • Route to the right rep — match leads to the best-positioned rep based on industry, size, deal complexity, geography, and existing relationships
  • Filter out noise — identify and suppress low-quality leads before they consume sales time

What makes it different

  • Criteria are yours, not defaults — the scoring framework is built against your actual buying criteria
  • Enrichment is continuous — when a new signal arrives, the agent updates the lead record and re-scores
  • Routing is multi-factor — considers deal complexity, rep capacity, existing relationships, and historical conversion patterns
  • Suppression is auditable — every filtered lead is logged with the reason

Case study: 80-100 qualified leads

A professional services firm was getting ~200 leads/month. Their sales team could only meaningfully engage about 40-50.

After deploying our lead qualification and enrichment engine:

  • Every lead is scored on fit criteria specific to their service offerings
  • Contact data is enriched automatically
  • High-score leads are routed to the right rep with full context
  • Low-quality leads are suppressed and logged

The sales team went from engaging 40-50 of 200 leads to engaging 80-100 of the highest-scoring leads — with better context and higher conversion rates.
