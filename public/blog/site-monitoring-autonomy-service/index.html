The three ways site issues cost you

  1. Lost revenue — broken checkout, broken forms, slow pages driving visitors away
  2. Damaged trust — visitors encountering errors, security warnings, or broken experiences
  3. Firefighting cost — emergency troubleshooting, weekend pages, reputation repair

Most teams discover problems after users report them. By then, the damage is done.

What Site Monitoring & Autonomy does

We deploy autonomous monitoring agents built on the Hermes Agent Platform that:

  • Run continuous audits — check for broken links, missing pages, performance regressions, accessibility issues, security misconfigurations
  • Monitor uptime and health — detect downtime, slow responses, and partial outages across your site and APIs
  • Detect regressions — compare current state against baselines and flag anything that degraded
  • Alert and escalate — notify the right people with specific details about what broke and where
  • Track trends — build a historical view of site health so you can see patterns and prioritize fixes

What makes it different

  • The fleet doesn't just alert — it fixes — common issues like broken links and misconfigured headers are fixed automatically
  • Audits run after every deploy — the monitoring agent audits your site after every deployment and compares against the pre-deploy baseline
  • It covers your APIs too — API endpoints, response times, error rates — all monitored continuously
