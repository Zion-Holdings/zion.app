AI for IT Vendor Management and Procurement

Vendor management and procurement contain repetitive review work: contract clauses, pricing models, renewal dates, and compliance evidence. AI can summarize risks, standardize evaluation, and accelerate approvals.

  • Automated RFP response drafting and evaluation
  • Vendor performance tracking and renewal alerts
  • Spend analysis and consolidation recommendations
  • Security and compliance evidence collection

Business value
