Most prospecting effort ends up on contacts that will never buy — wrong budget, wrong timeline, wrong need, wrong decision-maker.

When your team spends hours researching, crafting, and engaging bad-fit leads, that's time they're not spending on deals that could close.

What lead qualification agents do

  • Score leads continuously — evaluate fit across budget, authority, need, timeline, and other criteria relevant to your offering.
  • Enrich contact data — fill in missing fields (company size, tech stack, funding, key contacts) so your team has full context before the first touch.
  • Route to the right rep — match leads to the best-positioned rep based on industry, size, deal complexity, and existing relationships.
  • Filter out noise — identify and suppress low-quality leads before they consume sales time.

What this looks like in practice

A professional services firm was getting ~200 leads/month through their website and outbound efforts. Their sales team could only meaningfully engage about 40-50 of those.

After deploying a lead qualification and enrichment engine on the Hermes Agent Platform:

  • Every lead is scored on fit criteria specific to their service offerings
  • Contact data is enriched automatically (company size, industry, tech stack, key contacts)
  • High-score leads are routed to the right team member with full context
  • Low-quality leads are suppressed and logged

Result: the sales team went from engaging 40-50 of 200 leads to engaging 80-100 of the highest-scoring leads — with better context and higher conversion rates.
