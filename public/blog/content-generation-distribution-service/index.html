Today we're launching Content Generation & Distribution as a deployable service from Zion Tech Group. This is the fourth of five new Hermes Agent services.

Content is hard to sustain

Most companies want consistent content output but can't sustain it. The writers are busy with other work. The SEO specialist is overloaded. The social media manager is juggling too many channels.

Content generation and distribution agents built on the Hermes Agent Platform solve this by running a continuous content pipeline — research, draft, optimize, publish, distribute — with human review gates at the right points.

What Content Generation & Distribution does

We deploy content agents that manage the full pipeline:

  • Scan for coverage gaps — identify tools without blog coverage, service categories without landing pages, commercial-intent queries without guide content
  • Draft to standard template — write content using the standard post template with proper SEO markup
  • Optimize for SEO — apply proper heading hierarchy, internal linking, canonical URLs, Open Graph metadata, and schema markup
  • Verify publication — confirm the content appears in the right indexes
  • Regenerate the sitemap — after every publication, the sitemap is regenerated
  • Distribute across channels — push content to the right channels: blog, social media, RSS, newsletter

What makes it different

  • The agents know your site — they scan your existing catalog, identify gaps, and fill them in a way that's consistent with your existing content
  • Human review gates, not human execution — the agents do the research, drafting, optimization, and publication; humans review before final publication
  • Coverage gaps are identified automatically — the agents scan continuously and flag gaps as they appear

What this looks like in practice

A B2B technology company wanted consistent blog output — 2-3 posts per week — but their technical writers were already at capacity. They had 40+ tools and services but only a fraction had dedicated blog coverage.

After deploying our content generation pipeline:

  • Scans the site weekly for tools and services without blog coverage
  • Drafts posts for the highest-value gaps using the standard template
  • Verifies publication and regenerates the sitemap
  • Distributes to the blog and RSS feed
  • Flags posts for human review before final publication

Result: consistent blog output without adding headcount.
