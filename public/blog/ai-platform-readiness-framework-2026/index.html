AI Platform Readiness Framework 2026

Enterprise AI adoption fails on readiness, not algorithms. Assess your stack, data contracts, and operational model before committing to production.

Why readiness matters more than models

Most AI programs stall after the pilot. The cause is rarely the model. It is missing ownership, unclear data contracts, and an operational model that cannot enforce approvals or rollbacks. Zion's AI Platform Readiness Framework is a practical way to surface those gaps before you sign a large contract.

This guide is written for operators in fintech, healthcare, logistics, SaaS, and professional services — the same environments where Zion delivers automation.

The readiness layers

  • Data contracts: who owns the source, schema, quality, and refresh?
  • Model governance: evaluation criteria, drift checks, rollback rules, and release cadence.
  • Integration map: SaaS apps, APIs, legacy systems, and human handoffs.
  • Observability: logs, metrics, alerts, and an on-call path.
  • Security and compliance: access model, vendor posture, and evidence you already owe auditors.

What to measure

  • One trusted number for the decision you actually make weekly
  • Retrieval that cites sources instead of inventing policy
  • A backlog of automations ranked by impact × feasibility
