AI for IT Asset Lifecycle Management

IT asset management no longer has to rely on spreadsheets and manual audits. AI can automate discovery, entitlement reviews, patch compliance, and retirement workflows across endpoints, cloud services, and SaaS subscriptions.

Where AI adds the most value

  • Automated device discovery and ownership attribution
  • License compliance and unused seat detection
  • Predictive hardware failure and refresh timing
  • Automated offboarding and access revocation
  • Cost tracking, budgeting, and FinOps recommendations

Outcomes teams can expect

Faster inventory accuracy, fewer audit exceptions, lower SaaS waste, and smoother onboarding and offboarding. AI removes the repetitive work so IT can focus on strategic enablement.
