The problem with manual outreach

Most sales teams spend 60-80% of their time on activities that don't convert: researching leads manually, writing personalized outreach from scratch, following up inconsistently, tracking responses in spreadsheets.

Even when they do the work, they're bounded by hours in the day. A rep can send maybe 50-80 personalized emails before quality collapses.

What Auto-Outreach Automation does

We deploy autonomous outreach agents built on the Hermes Agent Platform that:

  • Research continuously — scan company databases, news, funding rounds, hiring patterns, and technographic signals to build a living lead pool
  • Craft personalized outreach — write context-aware messages that reference the prospect's actual situation
  • Manage multi-touch sequences — send the right message at the right time across email and messaging
  • Track and adapt — monitor responses, adjust messaging based on what works, route qualified leads to the right sales rep

What makes it different

  • Dedicated lead pool — verified B2B leads, not scraped lists
  • Dedicated sender reputation — outreach runs through a dedicated sending identity
  • Deduped sequences — never sends the same sequence twice
  • Reply handling — reads replies, classifies intent, routes accordingly

Case study: 3-4x pipeline

A mid-market SaaS company with 4 sales reps was generating ~15-20 qualified leads/month through manual outreach. After deploying our autonomous outreach engine:

  • Lead research went from manual to continuous (~500 prospects/day)
  • Outreach became personalized and context-aware
  • Multi-touch sequences ran automatically with value-add follow-ups
  • Qualified lead volume went from 15-20/month to 60-80/month in 60 days

Same 4 reps. 3-4x more pipeline. Agents handled research and admin.
