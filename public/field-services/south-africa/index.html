When to choose Priority vs NBD vs IMAC vs T&M vs FTE in South Africa

Priority / SBD is the right pick in South Africa when a production outage, POS failure, or executive-site break needs same-business-day arrival. The published Priority incident is $57.48 USD (transport + first hour); additional hours are $49.55 USD.

NBD fits degradations and next-day remediation — still urgent, but not revenue-stopping. An NBD incident in South Africa is $53.88 USD, with add-hours at $46.45 USD.

2BD IMAC covers install/move/add/change work where a two-business-day window is acceptable. Use it for desk moves, imaging waves, and staged rollouts at $49.20 USD per IMAC incident.

T&M is ideal for undefined scopes, audits, and surge projects: hourly $57.48 USD, daily $298.87 USD, weekly $1,075.95 USD, or monthly $3,012.66 USD.

FTE locks a named engineer capacity for South Africa — with backfill at $2,693.32 USD/month or without at $2,634.06 USD/month — suited to continuous onsite presence rather than discrete tickets.

Local delivery notes for South Africa

Business-hours dispatch is the default on the South Africa rate card. When engineers must work outside local business hours, an after-hours (OOH) uplift of 50% applies on top of the base SKU. Weekends and public holidays carry a 100% uplift. Both multipliers are quoted at booking — they are not separate Stripe products.

FAQ — field services in South Africa

What does an NBD incident cost in South Africa?

An NBD (next-business-day) incident in South Africa is $53.88 USD and includes transport plus the first hour onsite. Additional NBD hours are $46.45 USD.

How is Priority/SBD different from NBD in South Africa?

Priority/SBD targets same-business-day response for critical failures in South Africa at $57.48 USD per incident. NBD is the lower-cost next-day tier at $53.88 USD when overnight wait is acceptable.

Are weekend and after-hours rates higher in South Africa?

Yes. Outside business hours, a 50% uplift applies; weekends and holidays apply a 100% uplift on top of the base South Africa SKU you purchase.

Can I book T&M by the day in South Africa?

Yes. T&M daily in South Africa is $298.87 USD. Weekly ($1,075.95 USD) and monthly ($3,012.66 USD) blocks are also listed for longer surges.

When should South Africa customers choose FTE instead of tickets?

Choose FTE when South Africa needs continuous onsite capacity. With backfill the monthly rate is $2,693.32 USD; without backfill it is $2,634.06 USD. Otherwise stay on incident or T&M SKUs.

Is everything billed in USD for South Africa?

Yes. The Global Rate Card publishes South Africa prices in USD, and Stripe Payment Links charge USD. That supports USD payment links that reduce currency conversion friction.
