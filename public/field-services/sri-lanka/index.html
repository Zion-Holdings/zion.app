FAQ — field services in Sri Lanka

What local uplift applies to after-hours work in Sri Lanka?

For Sri Lanka, after-hours (OOH) work carries a 50% uplift on top of the base SKU. Weekends and public holidays carry a 100% uplift. Both are confirmed before dispatch — they are not separate Stripe products.

Are weekend field visits more expensive in Sri Lanka?

Yes. Weekend and holiday windows in Sri Lanka apply a 100% uplift, while non-weekend evenings use the 50% OOH uplift instead. Stacking rules are confirmed commercially at booking.

How should change managers in Sri Lanka budget OOH cutovers?

Start from the base SKU (Priority, NBD, IMAC, or T&M), then apply the Sri Lanka OOH uplift of 50% if the window is outside business hours, or 100% if it falls on a weekend/holiday.

When should Sri Lanka customers choose FTE instead of tickets?

Choose FTE when Sri Lanka needs continuous onsite capacity. With backfill the monthly rate is $4,181.73 USD; without backfill it is $4,089.73 USD.

What does an NBD incident cost in Sri Lanka?

An NBD (next-business-day) incident in Sri Lanka is $83.66 USD and includes transport plus the first hour onsite. Additional NBD hours are $72.12 USD.
