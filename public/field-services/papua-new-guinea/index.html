When to choose Priority vs NBD vs IMAC vs T&M vs FTE in Papua New Guinea

Priority / SBD is the right pick in Papua New Guinea when a production outage, POS failure, or executive-site break needs same-business-day arrival. The published Priority incident is $104.36 USD (transport + first hour); additional hours are $89.97 USD.

NBD fits degradations and next-day remediation — still urgent, but not revenue-stopping. An NBD incident in Papua New Guinea is $97.84 USD, with add-hours at $84.35 USD.

2BD IMAC covers install/move/add/change work where a two-business-day window is acceptable. Use it for desk moves, imaging waves, and staged rollouts at $89.33 USD per IMAC incident.

T&M is ideal for undefined scopes, audits, and surge projects: hourly $104.36 USD, daily $542.69 USD, weekly $1,953.70 USD, or monthly $5,470.35 USD.

FTE locks a named engineer capacity for Papua New Guinea — with backfill at $4,890.49 USD/month or without at $4,782.90 USD/month — suited to continuous onsite presence rather than discrete tickets.
