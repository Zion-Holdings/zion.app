When to choose Priority vs NBD vs IMAC vs T&M vs FTE in Thailand

Priority / SBD is the right pick in Thailand when a production outage, POS failure, or executive-site break needs same-business-day arrival. The published Priority incident is $54.45 USD (transport + first hour); additional hours are $46.94 USD.

NBD fits degradations and next-day remediation — still urgent, but not revenue-stopping. An NBD incident in Thailand is $51.05 USD, with add-hours at $44.01 USD.

2BD IMAC covers install/move/add/change work where a two-business-day window is acceptable. Use it for desk moves, imaging waves, and staged rollouts at $46.61 USD per IMAC incident.

T&M is ideal for undefined scopes, audits, and surge projects: hourly $54.45 USD, daily $283.14 USD, weekly $1,019.32 USD, or monthly $2,854.10 USD.

FTE locks a named engineer capacity for Thailand — with backfill at $2,551.56 USD/month or without at $2,495.43 USD/month — suited to continuous onsite presence rather than discrete tickets.

Local delivery notes for Thailand

Business-hours dispatch is the default on the Thailand rate card. When engineers must work outside local business hours, an after-hours (OOH) uplift of 50% applies on top of the base SKU. Weekends and public holidays carry a 100% uplift. Both multipliers are quoted at booking — they are not separate Stripe products.

FAQ — field services in Thailand

What does an NBD incident cost in Thailand?

An NBD (next-business-day) incident in Thailand is $51.05 USD and includes transport plus the first hour onsite. Additional NBD hours are $44.01 USD.

How is Priority/SBD different from NBD in Thailand?

Priority/SBD targets same-business-day response for critical failures in Thailand at $54.45 USD per incident. NBD is the lower-cost next-day tier at $51.05 USD when overnight wait is acceptable.

Are weekend and after-hours rates higher in Thailand?

Yes. Outside business hours, a 50% uplift applies; weekends and holidays apply a 100% uplift on top of the base Thailand SKU you purchase.

Can I book T&M by the day in Thailand?

Yes. T&M daily in Thailand is $283.14 USD. Weekly ($1,019.32 USD) and monthly ($2,854.10 USD) blocks are also listed for longer surges.

When should Thailand customers choose FTE instead of tickets?

Choose FTE when Thailand needs continuous onsite capacity. With backfill the monthly rate is $2,551.56 USD; without backfill it is $2,495.43 USD. Otherwise stay on incident or T&M SKUs.

Is everything billed in USD for Thailand?

Yes. The Global Rate Card publishes Thailand prices in USD, and Stripe Payment Links charge USD. That supports USD SKUs that keep regional controllers on one ledger.
