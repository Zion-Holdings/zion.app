Global IT Field Services Rate Card (USD)

Zion Tech Group’s Global Rate Card publishes onsite IT field-service prices for 231 countries in United States dollars. Each country page lists Priority / same-business-day (SBD) incidents, next-business-day (NBD) incidents, two-business-day IMAC (install/move/add/change), Time & Materials from hourly through monthly, and full-time equivalent (FTE) capacity with or without backfill. Every payable SKU carries a live Stripe Payment Link so procurement can book without waiting on a custom PDF.

SLAs are intentionally separated. Priority/SBD is for revenue-impacting or safety-adjacent outages that cannot wait overnight. NBD is the workhorse tier for degradations and standard break/fix that can close the next business day. 2BD IMAC is for planned change: desk moves, device swaps, imaging waves, and staged rollouts where a two-business-day window is enough. Mixing these tiers incorrectly is the most common way teams overspend — Priority tickets used for work that is really IMAC inflate cost without improving outcomes.

T&M versus FTE answers a different question: are you buying effort or capacity? T&M hourly, daily, weekly, and monthly SKUs fit audits, migrations, and surges with a visible burn rate. FTE (with or without backfill) fits sites that need a standing engineer presence. Convert from T&M to FTE once utilization stays high for several consecutive weeks; convert back when the surge ends.

OOH and weekend uplifts are multipliers, not separate products. When work falls outside local business hours, the after-hours uplift applies on top of the base SKU; weekends and public holidays apply the weekend/holiday uplift. Country pages show the percentages so change managers can forecast cutover cost before approving the window. All checkout remains in USD, which keeps multi-country field spend on one ledger for controllers in Americas, EMEA, APAC, and Africa clusters.

Discovery for $99 sits upstream of the rate card. Use Discovery when you are unsure whether the right next step is onsite field service, remote remediation, automation, or a packaged retainer. Discovery maps one process or site pattern, returns an executive readout, and includes a short working session — then you return here to purchase the matching country SKU, or move into Plans / Enterprise for multi-country programs.

FAQ

What is included in an incident price?

Incident SKUs include transport plus the first hour onsite for that SLA. Additional hours are purchased with the matching “Additional Hour” SKU for Priority, NBD, or IMAC.

Do you publish rates for every country in USD?

Yes. All 231 country pages on this hub show USD amounts for the 2026 Q3 Global Rate Card, with Stripe Payment Links on payable SKUs.

How do OOH and weekend uplifts work?

They multiply the base rate when work is outside business hours or on weekends/holidays. Percentages appear on each country page and are confirmed before dispatch.

When should I buy T&M instead of an incident?

Use T&M when scope is open-ended (audits, migrations, unknown effort). Use incident SKUs when the ticket class and first-hour envelope are clear.

What is the difference between FTE with and without backfill?

With backfill includes coverage when the primary engineer is unavailable. Without backfill is a lower monthly rate for sites that can tolerate gaps.

How does Discovery $99 fit before purchasing?

Discovery helps you decide if onsite field work is warranted versus remote fix or a packaged plan — then you purchase the appropriate country SKU from this hub.

Can I compare neighboring countries?

Yes. Use this hub’s country list, or jump between pages in the same regional cluster (Americas, EMEA, APAC, Africa). Rates differ by market even when SLA names match.
