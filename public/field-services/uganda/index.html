When to choose Priority vs NBD vs IMAC vs T&M vs FTE in Uganda

Priority / SBD is the right pick in Uganda when a production outage, POS failure, or executive-site break needs same-business-day arrival. The published Priority incident is $62.01 USD (transport + first hour); additional hours are $53.46 USD.

NBD fits degradations and next-day remediation — still urgent, but not revenue-stopping. An NBD incident in Uganda is $58.14 USD, with add-hours at $50.12 USD.

2BD IMAC covers install/move/add/change work where a two-business-day window is acceptable. Use it for desk moves, imaging waves, and staged rollouts at $53.08 USD per IMAC incident.

T&M is ideal for undefined scopes, audits, and surge projects: hourly $62.01 USD, daily $322.47 USD, weekly $1,160.89 USD, or monthly $3,250.50 USD.

FTE locks a named engineer capacity for Uganda — with backfill at $2,905.95 USD/month or without at $2,842.02 USD/month — suited to continuous onsite presence rather than discrete tickets.

Local delivery notes for Uganda

Business-hours dispatch is the default on the Uganda rate card. When engineers must work outside local business hours, an after-hours (OOH) uplift of 50% applies on top of the base SKU. Weekends and public holidays carry a 100% uplift. Both multipliers are quoted at booking — they are not separate Stripe products.

FAQ — field services in Uganda

What does an NBD incident cost in Uganda?

An NBD (next-business-day) incident in Uganda is $58.14 USD and includes transport plus the first hour onsite. Additional NBD hours are $50.12 USD.

How is Priority/SBD different from NBD in Uganda?

Priority/SBD targets same-business-day response for critical failures in Uganda at $62.01 USD per incident. NBD is the lower-cost next-day tier at $58.14 USD when overnight wait is acceptable.

Are weekend and after-hours rates higher in Uganda?

Yes. Outside business hours, a 50% uplift applies; weekends and holidays apply a 100% uplift on top of the base Uganda SKU you purchase.

Can I book T&M by the day in Uganda?

Yes. T&M daily in Uganda is $322.47 USD. Weekly ($1,160.89 USD) and monthly ($3,250.50 USD) blocks are also listed for longer surges.

When should Uganda customers choose FTE instead of tickets?

Choose FTE when Uganda needs continuous onsite capacity. With backfill the monthly rate is $2,905.95 USD; without backfill it is $2,842.02 USD. Otherwise stay on incident or T&M SKUs.

Is everything billed in USD for Uganda?

Yes. The Global Rate Card publishes Uganda prices in USD, and Stripe Payment Links charge USD. That supports USD payment links that reduce currency conversion friction.
