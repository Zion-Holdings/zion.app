FAQ — field services in Japan

What local uplift applies to after-hours work in Japan?

For Japan, after-hours (OOH) work carries a 50% uplift on top of the base SKU. Weekends and public holidays carry a 100% uplift. Both are confirmed before dispatch — they are not separate Stripe products.

Does Japan pricing include engineer transport?

Incident SKUs for Japan include transport plus the first hour. If the visit runs longer, purchase the matching additional-hour SKU rather than a second incident.

Is everything billed in USD for Japan field services?

Yes. The Global Rate Card publishes Japan prices in USD, and Stripe Payment Links charge USD. That supports USD catalog pricing for global IT ops teams.

Are weekend field visits more expensive in Japan?

Yes. Weekend and holiday windows in Japan apply a 100% uplift, while non-weekend evenings use the 50% OOH uplift instead. Stacking rules are confirmed commercially at booking.

Can I book T&M by the day for projects in Japan?

Yes. T&M daily in Japan is $1,510.10 USD. Weekly ($5,436.37 USD) and monthly ($15,221.85 USD) blocks are listed for longer surges.

What does 2BD IMAC cost on the Japan rate card?

A 2BD IMAC incident in Japan is $248.57 USD with add-hours at $214.28 USD. It is intended for planned installs/moves/adds/changes, not emergency restore.
