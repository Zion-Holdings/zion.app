When to choose Priority vs NBD vs IMAC vs T&M vs FTE in Western Sahara

Priority / SBD is the right pick in Western Sahara when a production outage, POS failure, or executive-site break needs same-business-day arrival. The published Priority incident is $142.18 USD (transport + first hour); additional hours are $122.57 USD.

NBD fits degradations and next-day remediation — still urgent, but not revenue-stopping. An NBD incident in Western Sahara is $133.29 USD, with add-hours at $114.91 USD.

2BD IMAC covers install/move/add/change work where a two-business-day window is acceptable. Use it for desk moves, imaging waves, and staged rollouts at $121.69 USD per IMAC incident.

T&M is ideal for undefined scopes, audits, and surge projects: hourly $142.18 USD, daily $739.32 USD, weekly $2,661.56 USD, or monthly $7,452.36 USD.

FTE locks a named engineer capacity for Western Sahara — with backfill at $6,662.41 USD/month or without at $6,515.84 USD/month — suited to continuous onsite presence rather than discrete tickets.
