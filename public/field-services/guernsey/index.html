When to choose Priority vs NBD vs IMAC vs T&M vs FTE in Guernsey

Priority / SBD is the right pick in Guernsey when a production outage, POS failure, or executive-site break needs same-business-day arrival. The published Priority incident is $119.49 USD (transport + first hour); additional hours are $103.01 USD.

NBD fits degradations and next-day remediation — still urgent, but not revenue-stopping. An NBD incident in Guernsey is $112.02 USD, with add-hours at $96.57 USD.

2BD IMAC covers install/move/add/change work where a two-business-day window is acceptable. Use it for desk moves, imaging waves, and staged rollouts at $102.28 USD per IMAC incident.

T&M is ideal for undefined scopes, audits, and surge projects: hourly $119.49 USD, daily $621.34 USD, weekly $2,236.84 USD, or monthly $6,263.16 USD.

FTE locks a named engineer capacity for Guernsey — with backfill at $5,599.26 USD/month or without at $5,476.08 USD/month — suited to continuous onsite presence rather than discrete tickets.

Local delivery notes for Guernsey

Business-hours dispatch is the default on the Guernsey rate card. When engineers must work outside local business hours, an after-hours (OOH) uplift of 50% applies on top of the base SKU. Weekends and public holidays carry a 100% uplift. Both multipliers are quoted at booking — they are not separate Stripe products.

FAQ — field services in Guernsey

What does an NBD incident cost in Guernsey?

An NBD (next-business-day) incident in Guernsey is $112.02 USD and includes transport plus the first hour onsite. Additional NBD hours are $96.57 USD.

How is Priority/SBD different from NBD in Guernsey?

Priority/SBD targets same-business-day response for critical failures in Guernsey at $119.49 USD per incident. NBD is the lower-cost next-day tier at $112.02 USD when overnight wait is acceptable.

Are weekend and after-hours rates higher in Guernsey?

Yes. Outside business hours, a 50% uplift applies; weekends and holidays apply a 100% uplift on top of the base Guernsey SKU you purchase.

Can I book T&M by the day in Guernsey?

Yes. T&M daily in Guernsey is $621.34 USD. Weekly ($2,236.84 USD) and monthly ($6,263.16 USD) blocks are also listed for longer surges.

When should Guernsey customers choose FTE instead of tickets?

Choose FTE when Guernsey needs continuous onsite capacity. With backfill the monthly rate is $5,599.26 USD; without backfill it is $5,476.08 USD. Otherwise stay on incident or T&M SKUs.

Is everything billed in USD for Guernsey?
