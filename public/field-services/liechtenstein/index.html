When to choose Priority vs NBD vs IMAC vs T&M vs FTE in Liechtenstein

Priority / SBD is the right pick in Liechtenstein when a production outage, POS failure, or executive-site break needs same-business-day arrival. The published Priority incident is $176.97 USD (transport + first hour); additional hours are $152.56 USD.

NBD fits degradations and next-day remediation — still urgent, but not revenue-stopping. An NBD incident in Liechtenstein is $165.90 USD, with add-hours at $143.02 USD.

2BD IMAC covers install/move/add/change work where a two-business-day window is acceptable. Use it for desk moves, imaging waves, and staged rollouts at $151.47 USD per IMAC incident.

T&M is ideal for undefined scopes, audits, and surge projects: hourly $176.97 USD, daily $920.22 USD, weekly $3,312.79 USD, or monthly $9,275.81 USD.

FTE locks a named engineer capacity for Liechtenstein — with backfill at $8,292.58 USD/month or without at $8,110.14 USD/month — suited to continuous onsite presence rather than discrete tickets.

Local delivery notes for Liechtenstein

Business-hours dispatch is the default on the Liechtenstein rate card. When engineers must work outside local business hours, an after-hours (OOH) uplift of 50% applies on top of the base SKU. Weekends and public holidays carry a 100% uplift. Both multipliers are quoted at booking — they are not separate Stripe products.
