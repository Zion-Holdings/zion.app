When to choose Priority vs NBD vs IMAC vs T&M vs FTE in Yemen

Priority / SBD is the right pick in Yemen when a production outage, POS failure, or executive-site break needs same-business-day arrival. The published Priority incident is $320.66 USD (transport + first hour); additional hours are $276.43 USD.

NBD fits degradations and next-day remediation — still urgent, but not revenue-stopping. An NBD incident in Yemen is $300.61 USD, with add-hours at $259.15 USD.

2BD IMAC covers install/move/add/change work where a two-business-day window is acceptable. Use it for desk moves, imaging waves, and staged rollouts at $274.46 USD per IMAC incident.

T&M is ideal for undefined scopes, audits, and surge projects: hourly $320.66 USD, daily $1,667.41 USD, weekly $6,002.66 USD, or monthly $16,807.46 USD.

FTE locks a named engineer capacity for Yemen — with backfill at $15,025.87 USD/month or without at $14,695.30 USD/month — suited to continuous onsite presence rather than discrete tickets.

Local delivery notes for Yemen

Business-hours dispatch is the default on the Yemen rate card. When engineers must work outside local business hours, an after-hours (OOH) uplift of 50% applies on top of the base SKU. Weekends and public holidays carry a 100% uplift. Both multipliers are quoted at booking — they are not separate Stripe products.

FAQ — field services in Yemen

What does an NBD incident cost in Yemen?

An NBD (next-business-day) incident in Yemen is $300.61 USD and includes transport plus the first hour onsite. Additional NBD hours are $259.15 USD.

How is Priority/SBD different from NBD in Yemen?

Priority/SBD targets same-business-day response for critical failures in Yemen at $320.66 USD per incident. NBD is the lower-cost next-day tier at $300.61 USD when overnight wait is acceptable.

Are weekend and after-hours rates higher in Yemen?

Yes. Outside business hours, a 50% uplift applies; weekends and holidays apply a 100% uplift on top of the base Yemen SKU you purchase.

Can I book T&M by the day in Yemen?

Yes. T&M daily in Yemen is $1,667.41 USD. Weekly ($6,002.66 USD) and monthly ($16,807.46 USD) blocks are also listed for longer surges.

When should Yemen customers choose FTE instead of tickets?

Choose FTE when Yemen needs continuous onsite capacity. With backfill the monthly rate is $15,025.87 USD; without backfill it is $14,695.30 USD. Otherwise stay on incident or T&M SKUs.

Is everything billed in USD for Yemen?

Yes. The Global Rate Card publishes Yemen prices in USD, and Stripe Payment Links charge USD. That supports USD billing preferred by regional procurement teams.
