FAQ — field services in Myanmar

What local uplift applies to after-hours work in Myanmar?

For Myanmar, after-hours (OOH) work carries a 50% uplift on top of the base SKU. Weekends and public holidays carry a 100% uplift. Both are confirmed before dispatch — they are not separate Stripe products.

How should change managers in Myanmar budget OOH cutovers?

Start from the base SKU (Priority, NBD, IMAC, or T&M), then apply the Myanmar OOH uplift of 50% if the window is outside business hours, or 100% if it falls on a weekend/holiday.

Does Myanmar pricing include engineer transport?

Incident SKUs for Myanmar include transport plus the first hour. If the visit runs longer, purchase the matching additional-hour SKU rather than a second incident.

Are weekend field visits more expensive in Myanmar?

Yes. Weekend and holiday windows in Myanmar apply a 100% uplift, while non-weekend evenings use the 50% OOH uplift instead. Stacking rules are confirmed commercially at booking.
