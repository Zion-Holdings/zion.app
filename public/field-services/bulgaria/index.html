When to choose Priority vs NBD vs IMAC vs T&M vs FTE in Bulgaria

Priority / SBD is the right pick in Bulgaria when a production outage, POS failure, or executive-site break needs same-business-day arrival. The published Priority incident is $108.90 USD (transport + first hour); additional hours are $93.88 USD.

NBD fits degradations and next-day remediation — still urgent, but not revenue-stopping. An NBD incident in Bulgaria is $102.10 USD, with add-hours at $88.01 USD.

2BD IMAC covers install/move/add/change work where a two-business-day window is acceptable. Use it for desk moves, imaging waves, and staged rollouts at $93.21 USD per IMAC incident.

T&M is ideal for undefined scopes, audits, and surge projects: hourly $108.90 USD, daily $566.29 USD, weekly $2,038.64 USD, or monthly $5,708.19 USD.

FTE locks a named engineer capacity for Bulgaria — with backfill at $5,103.12 USD/month or without at $4,990.86 USD/month — suited to continuous onsite presence rather than discrete tickets.

Local delivery notes for Bulgaria

Business-hours dispatch is the default on the Bulgaria rate card. When engineers must work outside local business hours, an after-hours (OOH) uplift of 50% applies on top of the base SKU. Weekends and public holidays carry a 100% uplift. Both multipliers are quoted at booking — they are not separate Stripe products.

FAQ — field services in Bulgaria

What does an NBD incident cost in Bulgaria?

An NBD (next-business-day) incident in Bulgaria is $102.10 USD and includes transport plus the first hour onsite. Additional NBD hours are $88.01 USD.

How is Priority/SBD different from NBD in Bulgaria?

Priority/SBD targets same-business-day response for critical failures in Bulgaria at $108.90 USD per incident. NBD is the lower-cost next-day tier at $102.10 USD when overnight wait is acceptable.

Are weekend and after-hours rates higher in Bulgaria?

Yes. Outside business hours, a 50% uplift applies; weekends and holidays apply a 100% uplift on top of the base Bulgaria SKU you purchase.

Can I book T&M by the day in Bulgaria?

Yes. T&M daily in Bulgaria is $566.29 USD. Weekly ($2,038.64 USD) and monthly ($5,708.19 USD) blocks are also listed for longer surges.

When should Bulgaria customers choose FTE instead of tickets?

Choose FTE when Bulgaria needs continuous onsite capacity. With backfill the monthly rate is $5,103.12 USD; without backfill it is $4,990.86 USD. Otherwise stay on incident or T&M SKUs.

Is everything billed in USD for Bulgaria?
