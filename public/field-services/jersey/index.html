When to choose Priority vs NBD vs IMAC vs T&M vs FTE in Jersey

Priority / SBD is the right pick in Jersey when a production outage, POS failure, or executive-site break needs same-business-day arrival. The published Priority incident is $179.99 USD (transport + first hour); additional hours are $155.16 USD.

NBD fits degradations and next-day remediation — still urgent, but not revenue-stopping. An NBD incident in Jersey is $168.74 USD, with add-hours at $145.47 USD.

2BD IMAC covers install/move/add/change work where a two-business-day window is acceptable. Use it for desk moves, imaging waves, and staged rollouts at $154.06 USD per IMAC incident.

T&M is ideal for undefined scopes, audits, and surge projects: hourly $179.99 USD, daily $935.95 USD, weekly $3,369.42 USD, or monthly $9,434.37 USD.

FTE locks a named engineer capacity for Jersey — with backfill at $8,434.33 USD/month or without at $8,248.78 USD/month — suited to continuous onsite presence rather than discrete tickets.

Local delivery notes for Jersey

Business-hours dispatch is the default on the Jersey rate card. When engineers must work outside local business hours, an after-hours (OOH) uplift of 50% applies on top of the base SKU. Weekends and public holidays carry a 100% uplift. Both multipliers are quoted at booking — they are not separate Stripe products.

FAQ — field services in Jersey

What does an NBD incident cost in Jersey?

An NBD (next-business-day) incident in Jersey is $168.74 USD and includes transport plus the first hour onsite. Additional NBD hours are $145.47 USD.

How is Priority/SBD different from NBD in Jersey?

Priority/SBD targets same-business-day response for critical failures in Jersey at $179.99 USD per incident. NBD is the lower-cost next-day tier at $168.74 USD when overnight wait is acceptable.

Are weekend and after-hours rates higher in Jersey?

Yes. Outside business hours, a 50% uplift applies; weekends and holidays apply a 100% uplift on top of the base Jersey SKU you purchase.

Can I book T&M by the day in Jersey?

Yes. T&M daily in Jersey is $935.95 USD. Weekly ($3,369.42 USD) and monthly ($9,434.37 USD) blocks are also listed for longer surges.

When should Jersey customers choose FTE instead of tickets?

Choose FTE when Jersey needs continuous onsite capacity. With backfill the monthly rate is $8,434.33 USD; without backfill it is $8,248.78 USD. Otherwise stay on incident or T&M SKUs.

Is everything billed in USD for Jersey?

Yes. The Global Rate Card publishes Jersey prices in USD, and Stripe Payment Links charge USD. That supports USD invoicing for multinational cost centers.
