When to choose Priority vs NBD vs IMAC vs T&M vs FTE in Palau

Priority / SBD is the right pick in Palau when a production outage, POS failure, or executive-site break needs same-business-day arrival. The published Priority incident is $102.85 USD (transport + first hour); additional hours are $88.67 USD.

NBD fits degradations and next-day remediation — still urgent, but not revenue-stopping. An NBD incident in Palau is $96.42 USD, with add-hours at $83.12 USD.

2BD IMAC covers install/move/add/change work where a two-business-day window is acceptable. Use it for desk moves, imaging waves, and staged rollouts at $88.03 USD per IMAC incident.

T&M is ideal for undefined scopes, audits, and surge projects: hourly $102.85 USD, daily $534.83 USD, weekly $1,925.38 USD, or monthly $5,391.07 USD.

FTE locks a named engineer capacity for Palau — with backfill at $4,819.62 USD/month or without at $4,713.59 USD/month — suited to continuous onsite presence rather than discrete tickets.

Local delivery notes for Palau

Business-hours dispatch is the default on the Palau rate card. When engineers must work outside local business hours, an after-hours (OOH) uplift of 50% applies on top of the base SKU. Weekends and public holidays carry a 100% uplift. Both multipliers are quoted at booking — they are not separate Stripe products.

FAQ — field services in Palau

What does an NBD incident cost in Palau?

An NBD (next-business-day) incident in Palau is $96.42 USD and includes transport plus the first hour onsite. Additional NBD hours are $83.12 USD.

How is Priority/SBD different from NBD in Palau?

Priority/SBD targets same-business-day response for critical failures in Palau at $102.85 USD per incident. NBD is the lower-cost next-day tier at $96.42 USD when overnight wait is acceptable.

Are weekend and after-hours rates higher in Palau?

Yes. Outside business hours, a 50% uplift applies; weekends and holidays apply a 100% uplift on top of the base Palau SKU you purchase.

Can I book T&M by the day in Palau?

Yes. T&M daily in Palau is $534.83 USD. Weekly ($1,925.38 USD) and monthly ($5,391.07 USD) blocks are also listed for longer surges.

When should Palau customers choose FTE instead of tickets?

Choose FTE when Palau needs continuous onsite capacity. With backfill the monthly rate is $4,819.62 USD; without backfill it is $4,713.59 USD. Otherwise stay on incident or T&M SKUs.

Is everything billed in USD for Palau?

Yes. The Global Rate Card publishes Palau prices in USD, and Stripe Payment Links charge USD. That supports USD checkout for regional and remote-site field work.
