When to choose Priority vs NBD vs IMAC vs T&M vs FTE in Colombia

Priority / SBD is the right pick in Colombia when a production outage, POS failure, or executive-site break needs same-business-day arrival. The published Priority incident is $77.14 USD (transport + first hour); additional hours are $66.50 USD.

NBD fits degradations and next-day remediation — still urgent, but not revenue-stopping. An NBD incident in Colombia is $72.32 USD, with add-hours at $62.34 USD.

2BD IMAC covers install/move/add/change work where a two-business-day window is acceptable. Use it for desk moves, imaging waves, and staged rollouts at $66.03 USD per IMAC incident.

T&M is ideal for undefined scopes, audits, and surge projects: hourly $77.14 USD, daily $401.12 USD, weekly $1,444.04 USD, or monthly $4,043.30 USD.

FTE locks a named engineer capacity for Colombia — with backfill at $3,614.71 USD/month or without at $3,535.19 USD/month — suited to continuous onsite presence rather than discrete tickets.

Local delivery notes for Colombia

Business-hours dispatch is the default on the Colombia rate card. When engineers must work outside local business hours, an after-hours (OOH) uplift of 50% applies on top of the base SKU. Weekends and public holidays carry a 100% uplift. Both multipliers are quoted at booking — they are not separate Stripe products.

FAQ — field services in Colombia

What does an NBD incident cost in Colombia?

An NBD (next-business-day) incident in Colombia is $72.32 USD and includes transport plus the first hour onsite. Additional NBD hours are $62.34 USD.

How is Priority/SBD different from NBD in Colombia?

Priority/SBD targets same-business-day response for critical failures in Colombia at $77.14 USD per incident. NBD is the lower-cost next-day tier at $72.32 USD when overnight wait is acceptable.

Are weekend and after-hours rates higher in Colombia?

Yes. Outside business hours, a 50% uplift applies; weekends and holidays apply a 100% uplift on top of the base Colombia SKU you purchase.

Can I book T&M by the day in Colombia?

Yes. T&M daily in Colombia is $401.12 USD. Weekly ($1,444.04 USD) and monthly ($4,043.30 USD) blocks are also listed for longer surges.

When should Colombia customers choose FTE instead of tickets?

Choose FTE when Colombia needs continuous onsite capacity. With backfill the monthly rate is $3,614.71 USD; without backfill it is $3,535.19 USD. Otherwise stay on incident or T&M SKUs.

Is everything billed in USD for Colombia?

Yes. The Global Rate Card publishes Colombia prices in USD, and Stripe Payment Links charge USD. That supports transparent USD rates that simplify cross-border IT budgets.
