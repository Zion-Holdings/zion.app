When to choose Priority vs NBD vs IMAC vs T&M vs FTE in Antigua & Barbuda

NBD fits degradations and next-day remediation — still urgent, but not revenue-stopping. An NBD incident in Antigua & Barbuda is $144.64 USD, with add-hours at $124.69 USD.

2BD IMAC covers install/move/add/change work where a two-business-day window is acceptable. Use it for desk moves, imaging waves, and staged rollouts at $132.05 USD per IMAC incident.
