When to choose Priority vs NBD vs IMAC vs T&M vs FTE in Norway

Priority / SBD is the right pick in Norway when a production outage, POS failure, or executive-site break needs same-business-day arrival. The published Priority incident is $228.39 USD (transport + first hour); additional hours are $196.89 USD.

NBD fits degradations and next-day remediation — still urgent, but not revenue-stopping. An NBD incident in Norway is $214.12 USD, with add-hours at $184.58 USD.

2BD IMAC covers install/move/add/change work where a two-business-day window is acceptable. Use it for desk moves, imaging waves, and staged rollouts at $195.49 USD per IMAC incident.

T&M is ideal for undefined scopes, audits, and surge projects: hourly $228.39 USD, daily $1,187.63 USD, weekly $4,275.48 USD, or monthly $11,971.35 USD.

FTE locks a named engineer capacity for Norway — with backfill at $10,702.39 USD/month or without at $10,466.93 USD/month — suited to continuous onsite presence rather than discrete tickets.

Local delivery notes for Norway

Business-hours dispatch is the default on the Norway rate card. When engineers must work outside local business hours, an after-hours (OOH) uplift of 50% applies on top of the base SKU. Weekends and public holidays carry a 100% uplift. Both multipliers are quoted at booking — they are not separate Stripe products.

FAQ — field services in Norway

What does an NBD incident cost in Norway?

An NBD (next-business-day) incident in Norway is $214.12 USD and includes transport plus the first hour onsite. Additional NBD hours are $184.58 USD.

How is Priority/SBD different from NBD in Norway?

Priority/SBD targets same-business-day response for critical failures in Norway at $228.39 USD per incident. NBD is the lower-cost next-day tier at $214.12 USD when overnight wait is acceptable.

Are weekend and after-hours rates higher in Norway?

Yes. Outside business hours, a 50% uplift applies; weekends and holidays apply a 100% uplift on top of the base Norway SKU you purchase.

Can I book T&M by the day in Norway?

Yes. T&M daily in Norway is $1,187.63 USD. Weekly ($4,275.48 USD) and monthly ($11,971.35 USD) blocks are also listed for longer surges.

When should Norway customers choose FTE instead of tickets?

Choose FTE when Norway needs continuous onsite capacity. With backfill the monthly rate is $10,702.39 USD; without backfill it is $10,466.93 USD. Otherwise stay on incident or T&M SKUs.

Is everything billed in USD for Norway?

Yes. The Global Rate Card publishes Norway prices in USD, and Stripe Payment Links charge USD. That supports predictable USD T&M and FTE bands for quarterly planning.
