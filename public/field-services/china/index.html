FAQ — field services in China

What local uplift applies to after-hours work in China?

For China, after-hours (OOH) work carries a 50% uplift on top of the base SKU. Weekends and public holidays carry a 100% uplift. Both are confirmed before dispatch — they are not separate Stripe products.

How should change managers in China budget OOH cutovers?

Start from the base SKU (Priority, NBD, IMAC, or T&M), then apply the China OOH uplift of 50% if the window is outside business hours, or 100% if it falls on a weekend/holiday.

Is everything billed in USD for China field services?

Yes. The Global Rate Card publishes China prices in USD, and Stripe Payment Links charge USD. That supports USD catalog pricing for global IT ops teams.

Are weekend field visits more expensive in China?

Yes. Weekend and holiday windows in China apply a 100% uplift, while non-weekend evenings use the 50% OOH uplift instead. Stacking rules are confirmed commercially at booking.

When should China customers choose FTE instead of tickets?

Choose FTE when China needs continuous onsite capacity. With backfill the monthly rate is $6,591.54 USD; without backfill it is $6,446.52 USD.

Can I book T&M by the day for projects in China?

Yes. T&M daily in China is $731.46 USD. Weekly ($2,633.24 USD) and monthly ($7,373.08 USD) blocks are listed for longer surges.
