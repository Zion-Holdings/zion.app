BAA & compliance posture

BAA path

If Zion will see PHI or ePHI, a Business Associate Agreement is on the path before production access. Discovery names whether a BAA is needed and who signs for you.

Access and audit

Least-privilege accounts, MFA, and a log of who touched what. We will not take standing admin in the EHR to “move faster.”

Your compliance owner stays named

Zion is the implementer and, on Growth, the ops partner. We are not your covered-entity officer and we do not replace counsel.

No certified-product claim

We do not mark a SKU as HIPAA certified. Audit readiness is a process you own; we help you run the IT and automation pieces. Longer write-up: HIPAA IT blog.

Safe AI — admin first

  1. Admin workflows only until approved — scheduling, intake routing, invoice exceptions, and policy Q&A grounded in documents you provide.
  2. No consumer models for PHI — dumping charts into an unsanctioned chatbot is out of scope. Full stop.
  3. Agents need a job, tools, and a stop rule — same governance as autonomous AI agents. Write access to the EHR waits for counsel.
  4. IT ops still come first — identity, backups, and logging are managed IT work. AI does not replace that layer.
