FAQ

Answers for delivery and adoption planning

How do we choose the right app to start with?

Start with one high-friction workflow that has clear business impact — usually revenue, support volume, or delivery speed.

How quickly can we launch a production-ready pilot?

Most teams launch an initial pilot in 2-4 weeks with KPI tracking and a pragmatic operations handoff.

How do you handle security and compliance requirements?

Security, privacy, and compliance are designed into the delivery plan, including SOC 2, GDPR, HIPAA, and EU AI Act controls where needed.

Can Zion integrate with our existing tools and data stack?

Yes. We design for existing systems, APIs, data pipelines, and team handoffs from day one.

What does ownership look like after go-live?

You own the system. We provide runbooks, training, and optional ongoing optimization.

Can we start with a single app or do we need a full bundle?

Start with one app. Bundles are optional accelerators once the first workflow is proven.
