How It Works

  • Words — Sequences of non-whitespace characters.
  • Characters — Total character count including spaces.
  • Characters (no spaces) — Total character count excluding whitespace.
  • Sentences — Counted by terminal punctuation marks (. ! ?).
  • Paragraphs — Blocks of text separated by blank lines.
  • Reading Time — Estimated at 200 words per minute (average adult).

All processing happens locally in your browser. Your text is never sent to any server.
