2026 M&A Due Diligence Checklist

A focused checklist for technology acquisitions: legal, financial, operational, and technical due diligence.

  • Financial statements and unit economics review
  • Cap table, equity, and liabilities audit
  • Customer contracts and concentration analysis
  • Revenue recognition policies and ARR quality
  • IP ownership, licenses, and open-source compliance
  • Security posture and incident history
  • Architecture, scalability, and tech debt review
  • Regulatory, compliance, and data residency
  • Employment agreements and key-person risk
  • Integration plan and post-close roadmap
