How It Works

  • Voices — Select from voices available in your browser. The list varies by device and installed language packs.
  • Speed — Adjust playback rate from 0.5x (slow) to 2x (fast).
  • Controls — Play starts speech, Pause resumes from where you stopped, Stop resets to the beginning.
  • Download — Generates an MP3 file using a free TTS service. Limited to 200 characters per download.
  • Privacy — Playback uses your browser's built-in speech engine. Your text stays on your device.

For longer texts, use the built-in playback. Download is best for short phrases and quotes.
