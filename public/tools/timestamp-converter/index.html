Quick Reference

  • Unix timestamp — Seconds since Jan 1, 1970 00:00:00 UTC.
  • 10 digits = seconds, 13 digits = milliseconds.
  • ISO 8601 — YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm:ss.sssZ (universal sortable format).
