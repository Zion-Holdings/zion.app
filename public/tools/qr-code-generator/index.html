About QR Codes

  • What it is — A two-dimensional barcode that stores information in a grid of black and white squares.
  • Common uses — URLs, contact info, WiFi credentials, payment links, product tracking.
  • Error correction — QR codes can still be read even if partially damaged (up to 30% for level H).
  • Capacity — Up to 7,089 numeric characters or 4,296 alphanumeric characters.

All generation happens locally in your browser. No data is transmitted.
