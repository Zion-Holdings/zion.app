Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans anytime?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. All plans are month-to-month. Cancel anytime with no penalty. We believe in earning your business every month.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, bank transfers, and PayPal. Enterprise customers can also pay via invoice.

Do you offer discounts for annual billing?

Yes. Annual billing comes with a 15% discount. Contact us for custom enterprise pricing.

What's included in the free consultation?

A 30-minute session with a senior IT consultant to discuss your needs, review your current setup, and identify quick wins.
