Authentication & Authorization Services

Identity is the new perimeter. Zion Tech Group designs and deploys authentication and authorization architectures that secure access without slowing down your teams.

What We Cover

Our authentication and authorization practice spans the full identity lifecycle — from workforce SSO and customer identity (CIAM) to privileged access management, just-in-time access, and continuous verification under a zero-trust model. We integrate with the platforms you already run and design custom flows for the ones you're building.

Single Sign-On (SSO) & Federation

We deploy SAML 2.0, OIDC, and OAuth 2.0-based SSO across workforce applications, reducing password fatigue and helpdesk load. Federation bridges legacy directories and modern IdPs so that migrations don't break existing access patterns.

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

Risk-based MFA that adapts to context — device posture, location, behavioral signals — rather than prompting on every login. We support TOTP, 푸시, WebAuthn/FIDO2, and certificate-based authentication, and we tune policies per application risk tier.

Privileged Access Management (PAM)

Secrets vaults, session recording, just-in-time elevation, and credential rotation for administrative and service accounts. PAM is deployed for infrastructure teams, databases, cloud consoles, and third-party vendor access.

Zero-Trust Access & Continuous Verification

Policy-driven access that evaluates identity, device, workload, and request context on every connection. We implement micro-segmentation, least-privilege RBAC/ABAC, and continuous access evaluation so that a compromised credential doesn't become a full compromise.

Customer Identity (CIAM)

Scalable identity for customer-facing applications: registration, social login, account linking, consent management, and consent revocation. Designed for throughput and compliance with privacy regulations.
