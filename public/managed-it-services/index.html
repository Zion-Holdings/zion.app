What belongs in the SLA

A written SLA ships with Growth. Discovery and Consulting name the targets; they do not invent uptime percentages we have not agreed in writing.

  1. Response — who picks up, in what window, and on which channel (email, Slack, ticket).
  2. Coverage hours — business hours vs on-call. 24/7 is a scoped add, not a default slogan.
  3. Restore — backup success checks and a tested restore path, not only a backup job that “ran.”
  4. Change control — who may patch, when, and how rollback is called.
  5. Escalation — Zion → your owner → vendor. No silent tickets.

How is this different from a traditional MSP?

No. The usual pattern is one overloaded internal owner plus Zion on the runbook. If you have no internal owner, Discovery will say so — Growth still needs a named customer contact.
