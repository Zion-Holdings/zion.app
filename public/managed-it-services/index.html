How is this different from a traditional MSP?

An MSP often owns the stack and the relationship with vendors. Co-managed means your team stays in the loop. We write the split in Discovery so finance can see what the retainer covers. See the mid-market guide.

Do you replace our internal IT person?

No. The usual pattern is one overloaded internal owner plus Zion on the runbook. If you have no internal owner, Discovery will say so — Growth still needs a named customer contact.
