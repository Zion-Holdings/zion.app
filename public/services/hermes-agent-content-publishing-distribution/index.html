Content Publishing & Distribution

Autonomous content pipeline — creation, SEO, distribution, performance.

  • Autonomous content creation
  • SEO optimization & continuous tuning
  • Multi-channel distribution
  • Performance tracking & dashboard

What it does: Hermes Agent content publishing & distribution creates, optimizes, and distributes content autonomously across your channels — blog, social, email, ads — while continuously tuning based on performance data.

Human experts review and approve content before it goes live, so quality stays high while speed and scale increase dramatically.

What's delivered

  • Autonomous content creation — blog posts, social posts, newsletters, case studies, whitepapers
  • SEO optimization — continuous tuning based on search data, internal linking, structured data
  • Multi-channel distribution — blog, LinkedIn, X, Telegram, Discord, WhatsApp, email, ads
  • Performance tracking — traffic, engagement, conversions, SEO rankings, and dashboard reporting
  • Human-in-the-loop review — content is reviewed and approved by human experts before publication

How it works

  1. Plan — identify content opportunities based on audience, SEO, and business goals
  2. Draft — create content autonomously using Hermes Agent agents
  3. SEO & review — optimize for SEO, check quality, and prepare for human review
  4. Approve — human experts review and approve content before publication
  5. Publish & distribute — publish to blog and distribute across channels
  6. Track & tune — monitor performance and continuously improve

Who it's for

Content publishing & distribution is for any company that wants to publish more content, faster, with higher quality — without hiring a large content team. Ideal for B2B services, consulting, IT, and software companies.
