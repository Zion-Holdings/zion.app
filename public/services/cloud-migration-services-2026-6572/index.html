Cloud Migration Services

Plan and execute migrations from on-premises and legacy environments to AWS, GCP, and Azure — discovery, migration, and validation, with zero guesswork.

Moving to the cloud is a business decision, not just an infrastructure project. Zion Tech Group's cloud migration services cover the full lifecycle: assess what you have, design the target architecture, migrate with minimal downtime, and validate that everything works afterward — so you can focus on what the cloud enables, not on what the migration breaks.

What we cover

  • Discovery and assessment: Inventory of your current environment — servers, databases, applications, dependencies, and constraints — with a clear picture of what can move, what must be re-architected, and what to retire.
  • Migration planning: Phased migration roadmap with risk assessments, downtime windows, rollback plans, and success criteria for each wave.
  • Execution: Lift-and-shift, replatform, or re-architect migrations to AWS, GCP, and Azure, handled by engineers who have done this before — not by theory.
  • Data migration: Database and file migration with integrity checks, sync strategies, and cutover planning to protect your data.
  • Post-migration validation: Functional testing, performance benchmarking, and operational readiness checks so the new environment is truly production-ready.

How it works

We start with a discovery engagement to understand your current state and goals. From there we produce a migration plan scoped to your environment, execute in controlled waves, and validate after each wave. You get a clear picture of progress, risk, and outcome at every step — no black-box migration.

Use cases

  • Datacenter exit: Move off aging on-premises infrastructure with a clear plan and a clean cutover.
  • Legacy modernization: Migrate legacy systems to cloud-native equivalents without rewriting everything from scratch.
  • Multi-cloud strategy: Distribute workloads across AWS, GCP, and Azure for resilience, cost, or vendor diversity.
  • Merger and acquisition integration: Consolidate migrated environments from acquired companies into a single cloud estate.
