Managed IT Services for Small Business

Ongoing IT support, infrastructure management, help desk, and proactive maintenance — so small teams get enterprise-grade IT without building an enterprise IT department.

Small businesses shouldn't have to choose between "we'll figure it out ourselves" and "we'll hire a full IT team we can't afford." Zion Tech Group's managed IT services for small business give you a real IT operation — support, maintenance, security, and planning — at a cost and commitment that makes sense for a small team.

What we cover

  • Help desk and support: A real support channel for your team when something breaks, doesn't work, or needs help — not a ticket system that goes nowhere.
  • Infrastructure management: Ongoing care for your network, servers, endpoints, cloud services, and the connections between them, so your environment stays healthy instead of waiting for something to fail.
  • Proactive maintenance: Patching, monitoring, backups, and routine checks that prevent problems rather than react to them — done on a schedule that fits your business.
  • Security operations: Baseline security for your environment: endpoint protection, access control, monitoring, and response when something looks wrong.
  • IT planning: Forward-looking guidance so your IT grows with your business instead of dragging it down — what to add, what to retire, what to budget for next.

How it works

We start by understanding your environment and what your team needs from IT day to day. From there we set up the management layer — monitoring, support access, maintenance cadence — and take over the routine work so your team can focus on the business. You get a single point of contact, predictable support, and an IT operation that behaves like it's yours — because it is.

Who it's for

Managed IT services from Zion are for small businesses that need reliable IT but can't justify a full in-house team — typically teams of a few to a few dozen people running on a mix of laptops, cloud services, and whatever infrastructure they've accumulated. If your IT is currently "whoever knows the most about computers," this is the upgrade.

Use cases

  • Growing small business: You've outgrown ad-hoc IT but aren't ready for a full internal department — get managed support that scales with you.
  • Distributed or remote teams: IT support and infrastructure management that works across locations and home offices, not just a single office.
  • Business without dedicated IT: Replace the "whoever knows computers" model with a real managed service and a clear support path.
  • IT offload for founders: Stop being the de facto IT person and get your team a real support function.
