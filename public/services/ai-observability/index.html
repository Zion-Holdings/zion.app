AI Observability

Metrics, traces, logs, anomaly detection, and AI-powered incident response for autonomous systems in production.

Zion Tech Group's AI observability services give you full visibility into how your autonomous systems behave in production — what they do, how they perform, and where they need attention — so you can operate with confidence instead of guesswork.

What we cover

  • Metrics and dashboards: Real-time visibility into system performance, throughput, latency, error rates, and resource usage across your AI workloads.
  • Distributed tracing: Follow individual requests and tasks through complex multi-step AI pipelines so you can see exactly where time is spent and where things go wrong.
  • Log aggregation and analysis: Centralized logs with intelligent search, correlation, and pattern detection — no more digging through scattered log files.
  • Anomaly detection: AI models that learn normal behavior and flag deviations early, before they become incidents your users notice.
  • AI-powered incident response: Automated triage, escalation, and initial response workflows that reduce mean time to resolution for operational issues.
  • Alerting and on-call integration: Smart alerts that reduce noise, route to the right responder, and include the context needed to act quickly.

How it works

We instrument your AI systems and autonomous agents with observability hooks, centralize the telemetry, and build the dashboards, alerts, and response workflows that match how your team operates. The goal is not more data — it's better visibility that leads to faster, better decisions.

Who it's for

AI observability from Zion is for organizations running autonomous systems in production — whether that's a single agent handling a critical workflow or a fleet of agents across multiple zones. If you need to know what your AI is doing, how well it's doing it, and what to do when it doesn't go as planned, this is the service.
