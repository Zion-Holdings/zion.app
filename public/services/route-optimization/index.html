AI Route Optimization

Optimize routes for delivery, field service, logistics, and transportation — multi-stop planning, real-time rerouting, constraint handling, and cost-efficient navigation powered by AI.

Routing Is a Hard Optimization Problem

Given 50 stops, 5 vehicles, time windows, traffic, driver shifts, vehicle capacity, and priority orders, how do you assign stops to vehicles and sequence them? The number of possible solutions is astronomical. Trying to solve this with spreadsheets and intuition leaves significant efficiency on the table — longer routes, more fuel, more overtime, more missed windows.

AI route optimization solves these problems systematically.

What the Service Covers

  • Multi-stop route planning — Plan routes that visit many stops across multiple vehicles, optimizing for total distance, time, or cost while respecting constraints.
  • Constraint handling — Time windows, vehicle capacity, driver hours, service duration, priority orders, skills requirements, and custom business rules. Routes respect reality.
  • Real-time rerouting — When traffic, weather, order changes, or breakdowns happen, re-optimize routes dynamically. Drivers get updated navigation; customers get updated ETAs.
  • Last-mile optimization — Optimize delivery routes for cost, time, and customer experience. Reduce miles driven, fuel consumed, and delivery failures.
  • Field service routing — Route technicians to service calls, balancing travel time, skill match, parts availability, and scheduled windows.
  • What-if analysis — Model the impact of adding stops, changing order volume, or modifying fleet size before making operational decisions.

Expected Outcomes

Fewer miles driven, lower fuel costs, more stops per vehicle per day, fewer missed delivery windows, and reduced driver overtime.
