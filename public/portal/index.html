Resources and program information for delivery partners, integrators, and providers.

Program overview

Zion Tech Group partners with integrators, managed service providers, and technology partners to deliver AI and IT solutions. The partner portal provides access to shared resources, program guidelines, and cooperation materials.

Partner types

Integrators — Implementation and delivery partners for complex AI and IT programs.
Providers — Managed service providers and platform partners with outcome-based agreements.
Technology partners — Tooling and platform vendors aligned with Zion delivery practices.

Getting started

New partners should contact our partnerships team to discuss fit, scope, and operating model. Existing partners can reach their assigned relationship manager through the contact form.
