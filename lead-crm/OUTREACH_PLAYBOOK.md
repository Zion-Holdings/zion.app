- Incident response retainer: autonomous triage + human escalation.
- FinOps cost governance: tagging, budgets, shutdown/scale automation.

## CEO reply rules
- Language: match the conversation language when possible; default to Portuguese.
- Opening: thank them for the past project/opportunity.
- Body: 1-2 concrete mutually beneficial next steps tied to the angle above.
- CTA: schedule a meeting at https://calendly.com/kleber-ziontechgroup
- CTA: visit https://ziontechgroup.com for free services and tools.
- Tone: friendly, professional, not overly formal.
- Length: ~120-180 words.
- Closing: Kleber Garcia Alcatrão, +1 302 464 0950.

## Dedupe rules
- Suppress if recipient is in pipeline_sent_cache.txt within 7 days.
- Suppress if message is from our own addresses.
- Suppress system/no-reply senders.

## Sequencing
- Day 0: CEO warm intro using past project reference.
- Day 2: value-first follow-up with one quick-win offer.
- Day 5: CTA-focused close with Calendly + free resources.
