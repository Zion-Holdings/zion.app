# Case Study: AI Customer Support Automation for Enterprise SaaS

## Client Profile

**Industry:** Enterprise SaaS  
**Company Size:** $50M ARR  
**Challenge:** High support ticket volume and poor SLA compliance

## The Challenge

An enterprise SaaS company was facing:
- 15,000+ support tickets per month
- 38% first-contact resolution rate
- 24+ hour average response time
- $2.3M annual support costs

## Our Solution

Deployed **AI Customer Support Pro** with:

1. **Intelligent triage** - Automatic ticket routing and categorization
2. **Automated responses** - 80% of inquiries resolved without human intervention
3. **Sentiment analysis** - Priority escalation for negative sentiment
4. **Knowledge base integration** - Context-aware responses

## Results

| Metric | Before | After | Improvement |
|--------|--------|-------|-------------|
| First-contact Resolution | 38% | 71% | **87% increase** |
| Average Response Time | 24 hours | 2.1 hours | **91% reduction** |
| Support Ticket Volume | 15,000/mo | 8,200/mo | **45% reduction** |
| Support Costs | $2.3M/yr | $1.4M/yr | **$900K savings** |

## Business Impact

- **64% faster support resolution**
- **$900,000 annual cost reduction**
- **45% fewer support tickets** through self-service
- **99.9% SLA compliance** achieved

## ROI Summary

**18-month payback period** with ongoing 40% cost savings year-over-year.
