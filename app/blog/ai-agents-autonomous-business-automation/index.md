## What Are AI Agents?

AI agents are autonomous software systems that can:
- **Perceive** their environment through data and APIs
- **Reason** about problems using large language models
- **Act** by executing tasks, writing code, and making decisions
- **Learn** from results to improve future performance

Unlike traditional automation, AI agents can handle novel situations, adapt to changing requirements, and operate at scale without explicit programming for every scenario.
